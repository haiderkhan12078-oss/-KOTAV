Argus / controlled execution

Repeatable testing. Reviewed conclusions.

Argus is the automation layer: it executes approved checks, preserves the evidence trail and routes material signals into specialist review before delivery.

The operating model

Fast automation without pretending software replaces judgment.

Argus handles repeatable work inside the signed boundary. Security specialists reproduce material candidates, remove false positives and add the business and remediation context that automated output alone cannot provide.

  • Runs only against authorised targets
  • Preserves requests, responses and reproduction evidence
  • Keeps candidate signals separate from verified findings
  • Supports portable outputs such as SARIF where applicable
Controlled by scope.No destructive testing or out-of-bound probing without explicit authorisation.
Engagement flow

From decision to closure.

A credible assessment is more than “run scanner, export PDF.” The workflow needs ownership at every stage.

01

Scope

02

Authorise

03

04

Deliver + Retest
